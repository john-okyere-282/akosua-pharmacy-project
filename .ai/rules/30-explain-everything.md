# Architectural Transparency & Explainability Rules

- For every design choice, record:
  1. Options considered (2-3 realistic options)
  2. Decision made
  3. Why this choice fits Akosua's pharmacy
  4. Trade-offs accepted
- Write documents in the first person ("I chose...", "I considered..."), clear and grounded, without artificial jargon.
- Stop and seek decisions for major forks rather than making silent assumptions.
